Patient Guide

Prepared, Comfortable, Confident

A first visit goes more smoothly when you know what to expect. Alongside the answers below, this general checklist can help you prepare for your consultation.

  • Note down any dental concerns or questions in advance
  • Bring a list of any current medicines you take
  • Arrive a few minutes early for first registrations
  • Carry any previous dental records you may have, if relevant
